⚙️ The Engineering Ethos

"Don't Chase Trends. Chase Depth."

The tech world is drowning in surface-level AI prompt-wrappers and no-code buzzwords. Anyone can ask an LLM to spit out a generic landing page with purple gradients.

Real engineering begins when the user actually hits the system:

  • What happens when two people buy the very last seat on Ojaja Cinemas at the exact same millisecond?
  • What happens when Deriv WebSockets drop a frame during high-impact US Non-Farm Payroll news?
  • What happens when a food vendor's network in Osogbo blips during a payment webhook?

Because I studied Mechanical Engineering, I think about stress limits, continuous friction, and fail-safe valves before I write a single line of backend logic. That is the difference between a prototype that breaks and software that lasts.
