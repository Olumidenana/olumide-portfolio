Executive Profile

I am a Full-Stack Systems Engineer and Algorithmic Architect with a background in Mechanical Engineering from Obafemi Awolowo University (OAU). I view software through the lens of mechanical systems under load—focusing on stress limits, friction reduction, concurrency bottlenecks, and strict fail-safes.

Over the past several years, I transitioned from operational process automation to designing and shipping high-concurrency, low-latency production platforms spanning quantitative trading execution, high-throughput merchant fintech operating systems, spatial community networks, and distributed cloud media rendering.

My engineering work is defined by depth over buzzwords: zero-lookahead algorithmic event routing, PLpgSQL row-level locking to prevent double-spending, C FFI bridges to MetaTrader 4 & 5, and in-browser 3D/WebGL experiences that eliminate expensive cloud software fees for creators.
